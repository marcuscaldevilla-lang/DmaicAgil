---
name: AI financial suggestions
description: Safety boundary for Charter financial guidance generated alongside the DMAIC pipeline.
---

Financial contribution and gain suggestions shown in the Charter must be server-controlled validation guidance, never model-provided monetary claims. Apply the same normalization when generating a pipeline, saving pending suggestions, and reading legacy suggestions.

**Why:** A prompt alone cannot prove that a language model will not label a fabricated financial amount as confirmed. Persisted suggestions can outlive later prompt changes, so read-time normalization is also required.

**How to apply:** Keep team-entered financial information factual and editable. If changing the generated Charter or workspace serialization, preserve the controlled guidance for business contributions, expected savings, and financial gain unless a reviewed, structured provenance model replaces it.
